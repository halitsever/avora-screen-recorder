# How to screen record on Mac

<p class="post-meta">By <a href="https://halit.org">Halit Sever</a> · <time datetime="2026-11-28">28 November 2026</time></p>

<p class="lead">Every Mac can record its screen without installing anything. The Screenshot toolbar records the whole screen, a window or an area, with your microphone. Here is how, and when a free app such as Avora is worth adding.</p>

## With the Screenshot toolbar

1. Press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>5</kbd>. The Screenshot toolbar opens at the bottom of the screen.
2. Pick what to record: **Record the entire screen**, **Record a window** or **Record a portion of the screen**. For a portion, drag the frame to the area you want.
3. Open **Options** to choose a microphone, a timer, where the file is saved, and whether to show mouse clicks.
4. Click **Record**. For the entire screen you can also click anywhere on the screen.
5. To stop, click the Stop button in the menu bar or press <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

Recording a single window arrived in macOS Tahoe 26. On macOS 27 Golden Gate the Options menu also has **Include System Audio**, so the Mac's own sound can be recorded without extra software. The file is a .mov on the desktop unless you chose another place.

The macOS steps were checked against Apple's [macOS User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/take-screenshots-or-screen-recordings-mh26782/mac) for macOS 27 Golden Gate and [Apple's screen recording guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102618) in November 2026.

## With QuickTime Player

Open QuickTime Player and choose **File › New Screen Recording**, or press <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>. On current macOS this opens the same Screenshot toolbar, so the steps above apply.

## The permission prompt

The first time an app records the screen, macOS asks for permission. Allow it in **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording**. The microphone and camera are separate permissions that are asked for the first time they are used.

## With Avora

The built-in recorder gives you the raw screen. [Avora](/) is a free, open source recorder for Mac that edits the video for you while it records:

- **Auto zoom.** Every click zooms the video in on that spot, and the camera follows the area you work in. [How it works](/blog/auto-zoom-screen-recording).
- **A cleaner cursor.** Avora draws the cursor itself, larger and with smoothed movement, with optional click effects.
- **Sound.** Your microphone and the Mac's own audio on separate tracks, mixed on export. See [how to record screen and audio](/blog/how-to-record-screen-and-audio-on-mac).
- **Camera and captions.** A camera bubble that steps aside when the screen zooms, and captions written on the Mac from what you say.
- **Export.** MP4 (H.264 or HEVC), ProRes 422 HQ or GIF, in the recording's own shape or 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 4:5, 3:4 or 9:16.

To record, pick **Whole Screen**, **Window** or **Area** under **Capture** in Avora's menu bar menu, then choose **Record Screen** or press <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> in any app. Press it again to stop. Avora needs macOS 14 or later, and the first recording asks for Screen Recording permission. [Download Avora](https://github.com/halitsever/avora-screen-recorder/releases/latest) from GitHub; the [home page](/#install) has the install steps.

## Which one to use

| You want | Use |
| --- | --- |
| A quick clip to send someone | The Screenshot toolbar |
| Voice-over on a recent Mac, nothing else | The Screenshot toolbar with a microphone |
| A product demo or tutorial with zoom, cursor and captions done for you | Avora |
| Live streaming or complex scenes | OBS Studio; see [OBS alternatives for Mac](/blog/obs-alternatives-for-mac) |

## FAQ

### How do I screen record on a Mac with sound?

Choose a microphone under Options in the Screenshot toolbar. For the Mac's own sound, macOS 27 Golden Gate adds Include System Audio; on older versions use an app such as Avora that captures it.

### Is screen recording on Mac free?

Yes. The Screenshot toolbar and QuickTime Player come with macOS. Avora is also free and open source.
