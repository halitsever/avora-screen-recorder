# How to stop a screen recording on Mac

<p class="post-meta">By <a href="https://halit.org">Halit Sever</a> · <time datetime="2026-11-28">28 November 2026</time></p>

<p class="lead">A Mac screen recording keeps going until you stop it. There are two ways to stop the built-in recorder: a button in the menu bar and a keyboard shortcut. Both work whether you started from the Screenshot toolbar or from QuickTime Player.</p>

## Stop the built-in recorder

- **Click the Stop button in the menu bar.** While recording, a small stop button (a square in a circle) sits near the right end of the menu bar. Click it once.
- **Or press <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Esc</kbd>.** This stops the recording from the keyboard, which is handy when the menu bar is hidden.

The recording is saved as soon as it stops. By default it goes to the desktop as a .mov file named "Screen Recording" plus the date and time, and a thumbnail appears in the corner for a few seconds. To cancel before recording starts, press <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

The macOS steps were checked against Apple's [macOS User Guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/take-screenshots-or-screen-recordings-mh26782/mac) for macOS 27 Golden Gate and [Apple's screen recording guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102618) in November 2026.

## QuickTime Player recordings

In QuickTime Player, **File › New Screen Recording** (<kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>N</kbd>) opens the same Screenshot toolbar, so the recording is stopped the same way: the Stop button in the menu bar or <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

## The Stop button is not there

- **An app is in full screen.** The menu bar hides. Move the pointer to the top edge of the screen to show it, or press <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Esc</kbd>.
- **The menu bar is set to hide automatically.** Move the pointer to the top edge of the screen to bring it back.
- **Too many menu bar icons.** On a small screen, icons can be pushed under the camera notch. The keyboard shortcut still works.
- **You recorded with another app.** Third-party recorders have their own stop control. Check that app's icon in the menu bar or its window.

## Stop, pause or restart in Avora

[Avora](/) is a free, open source screen recorder for Mac (macOS 14 or later). It lives in the menu bar and can be stopped three ways:

- Choose **Stop Recording** from Avora's menu bar menu.
- Press <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd> in any app. The same shortcut starts a recording.
- Click **Stop** in Avora's own window, if it is open. The button shows how long you have been recording.

While recording, <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>P</kbd> pauses and resumes, and the video, the sound and the clicks all skip the paused time together. <kbd>Control</kbd> <kbd>Option</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Delete</kbd> throws the take away and starts again, and **Discard Recording…** in the menu drops it without saving.

When Avora stops, it renders the video with the automatic zoom and your chosen look, then saves it to `~/Movies/Avora` and opens Finder on it, or copies it to the clipboard, depending on **After Recording** in **Settings…**. More on that in [where screen recordings go on Mac](/blog/where-do-screen-recordings-go-on-mac).

## FAQ

### What is the shortcut to stop screen recording on a Mac?

Command-Control-Esc stops a recording made with the Screenshot toolbar or QuickTime Player.

### Why can't I stop my screen recording on Mac?

The Stop button lives in the menu bar, which hides when an app is in full screen. Move the pointer to the top of the screen or press Command-Control-Esc.

### Does stopping a recording save it automatically?

Yes. The built-in recorder saves the file as soon as you stop, to the desktop unless you chose another place under Options.
