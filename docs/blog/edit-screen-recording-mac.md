# Editing a screen recording on a Mac

<p class="post-meta">By <a href="https://halit.org">Halit Sever</a> · <time datetime="2026-11-28">28 November 2026</time></p>

<p class="lead">Most screen recordings need a little work before they are shared: cut the start where you found the right window, remove a mistake, speed up a slow part. QuickTime Player covers the cuts. For zooms, blurs and captions you need an editor built for screen recordings.</p>

## Quick edits in QuickTime Player

QuickTime Player opens the .mov that the built-in recorder saves and can:

- **Trim** the start and end: **Edit › Trim**, drag the yellow handles, click **Trim**.
- **Split** a movie into clips and **rearrange** them.
- **Rotate or flip** a clip, and **remove** the audio or the video.

Apple documents each of these in the [QuickTime Player user guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/quicktime-player/welcome/mac). Then **File › Export As** saves the result as a .mov at 4K, 1080p, 720p or 480p. There is no zoom, no blur, no captions and no speed change.

## Avora's editor

[Avora](/) is a free, open source screen recorder for Mac with an editor for its own recordings. Open the latest one with **Edit Last Recording…** (<kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>) from the menu bar menu. Raw recordings are kept for 7 days, so you can come back and export a take again with other settings. What the editor does:

| Task | How |
| --- | --- |
| Cut out a mistake | Put the playhead on it, press **S** to **Split**, select the piece and choose **Cut This Clip** |
| Speed up a slow part | Select the clip and pick a **Speed**: 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2×, 3×, 4× or 8×. Voices keep their pitch |
| Speed up typing only | **Speed Up Typing** plays every stretch of steady typing at 3× |
| Fix the zooms | Zooms are made from your clicks. Change or delete one in the zoom lane, or **Reset Zooms** to start over |
| Show only part of the screen | **Crop** and drag the frame |
| Hide private details | **Auto Blur** finds faces or private text, or **Add Blur** by hand. See [blur faces in a video](/blog/blur-faces-in-video) |
| Add captions | **Captions** writes them from what you said, on the Mac. See [add captions to a video](/blog/add-captions-to-video-mac) |
| Undo | <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd>, redo with <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd> |

Play and pause with <kbd>Space</kbd>, and step 0.1 seconds with the arrow keys.

## Export

Click **Export** (<kbd>Command</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>) to save a new video, or **Export and Copy** to put it on the clipboard as well. The format and shape come from the export settings: MP4 (H.264 or HEVC), ProRes 422 HQ or GIF, from 16:9 to 9:16. **Copy Frame** and **Save Frame…** save a still image from the playhead.

Avora needs macOS 14 or later. [Download Avora](https://github.com/halitsever/avora-screen-recorder/releases/latest).

## FAQ

### Can I edit a screen recording in QuickTime?

Yes, for simple edits: trim, split, rearrange, rotate and remove audio. It cannot zoom, blur, speed up or add captions.

### How do I cut the middle out of a screen recording?

In QuickTime, split the movie into clips and delete the one you don't want. In Avora, split at the start and end of the mistake with S and choose Cut This Clip.

### Can I speed up part of a screen recording?

Avora can: select a clip and choose a speed up to 8x, or use Speed Up Typing to play typing at 3x. QuickTime Player has no speed control for exports.
